How to install

1

Tap Download the App above. If your browser asks, choose Download anyway.

2

Open the downloaded bodymarketlabs.apk file from your Downloads folder.

3

If Android displays “For your security…”, tap Settings, enable Allow from this source, return to the installer and tap Install.

4

Open the app and sign in. Future updates can be installed from inside the app.
